About Me

I am currently a junior at Arizona State University, where I am majoring in Interior Design and minoring in both Business and Sustainability. I was born in Northern Montana and raised in Arizona's West Valley. I love Arizona and all it has to offer; I love the desert landscape and using it to enhance my projects to bring the beauty of the desert indoors.

My passion for interior design was founded quite early in life; I enjoyed playing games like Minecraft. As early as the third grade, I was sorting my shopkins into families and making floor plans for them based on what I felt their families might value and want in their lives. My parents suggested architecture as a potential career path due to my interest in Minecraft, and come my 12th birthday, I was gifted a book about architecture and about how to start in that field. After exploring the book and practicing, I quickly realized that architecture was not the path my passion and desire was leading me down. Back to square one, I discovered the show that would truly ignite my desire to become an interior designer: Fixer Upper.

This HGTV show, starring Chip and Joanna Gaines, follows this young couple from Texas as they renovate homes for their clients, turning these "fixer uppers" into their dream homes. Chip was most of the brawn behind the operations, leading the construction crew in demo and renovation work, while Joanna brought her design expertise to create beautiful, functional spaces. I was immediately drawn to Joanna's work, and quickly realized that she was doing what all passion and ambition was leading me to. Watching her process inspired me to pursue a career in interior design, where I could combine my love for aesthetics with my desire to help others create their ideal living environments.

This discovery came about in seventh grade, and since then it has only grown. Throughout high school I explored interior design and what it all entailed. At this time, I was still simply considering aesthetics, but I was able to start distinguishing interior design from interior decorating; and I knew I wanted to do the former. Once in college, I began to discover the true depth and thought that goes into interior design, and I started falling in love more and more every day. I also began to pursue a minor in business starting my freshman year, hoping to someday apply those principles and skills to my life and even my own business someday. As my college career continued, I began hearing and learning more about sustainability and sustainable practices, and I was interested in learning more, so during my sophomore year, I added a sustainability minor, and I am really hoping to use the knowledge I learn there in my field and eventually get my LEED certification.
